# Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights  

## 📊 Project Overview  
This project analyses 2011 retail sales data to deliver actionable insights for senior leadership at a global retail client.  
The goal was to support **strategic decision-making** by identifying revenue trends, high-value customers, and international markets with strong expansion potential.  

The work was done using **Power BI** and presented as an executive dashboard.  

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
1. Identify seasonal revenue trends for 2011 to improve forecasting.  
2. Compare revenue and demand (quantity sold) across the **top 10 countries**, excluding the UK.  
3. Highlight the **top 10 customers by revenue** to support retention strategies.  
4. Visualise global product demand to inform **market expansion opportunities**.  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Skills Applied  
- **Data Cleaning & Transformation** → validated unit price, quantity, and customer IDs.  
- **Power BI** → created interactive dashboards and executive visuals.  
- **Data Analysis & Storytelling** → framed insights for strategic decision-making.  
- **Business Consulting Approach** → communicated findings in a client-facing style.

## 🔑 Key Insights  
- **Revenue Trends** → Revenue peaked in **November 2011 ($1.5M)** with seasonal spikes in Q4, guiding inventory and marketing strategies.  
- **Top Countries** → The **Netherlands ($285k)**, **EIRE ($283k)**, and **Germany ($228k)** were leading markets (excluding UK).  
- **Top Customers** → Customer **14646 ($279k)** and **18102 ($256k)** accounted for a large share of revenue, highlighting the importance of client retention.  
- **Global Demand** → The **Netherlands (27k+ units)**, EIRE, and Germany show high demand, making them prime targets for expansion.
